        How to play

        Rocky runs along a live seismograph, and every jump is written to the trace. Get as far as you can. Your score is the Magnitude, worked out from your distance and your shards on a Richter-style scale, from M 1 Micro up to M 8 Great. Three cracks and Rocky shatters.

        Left-handed? Settings swaps the two sides of the screen. A stomp smashes the Watchers (not the ones firing a beam), Moths, Probes, Burrowers and Rocks it hits. Its landing shockwave shatters anything stompable nearby (not Moths, which only a direct hit breaks), Golems and Spires too, for bonus shards. Don't land right on top of a Golem or a Spire.

        How a run works

        • Every 600 m an aftershock hits: the run speeds up (up to a top speed), and every second one takes you to the next district (Bank District, The Exchange, Glass City, then The Enclave for good).
        • From the second aftershock on, every other one brings a set piece: Tremor, Watcher swarm, Falling masonry or Glass storm.
        • Passing a hazard by a hair is a close call: +2 shards, more with a chain.
        • In each run, the first time a hazard shows up, a hint says what to do. Settings can turn hints off.

        Modes, missions and the leaderboard

        • Endless: your best run goes on the all-time board. Daily: one course for everyone, new every day at 00:00 UTC, with its own board.
        • You always have three missions. Finishing them raises your Magnitude rank (1.0 to 9.0) and unlocks skins for Rocky at ranks 3, 5, 7 and 9.
        • Your runner name is your place on the board and the key to your save. Next time, on this phone or any other device, enter the same name to get your rank, missions, skins and best runs back.
